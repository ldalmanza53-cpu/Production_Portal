Student 1 • Class 37 Focus

Maya Lin

Lead Investigative Journalist

Persona: A tenacious news reporter breaking live, unverified stories to the public.

Mission: Report recently completed actions and ongoing unverified developments regarding the shutdown.

Target Language Checklist:
  • Present perfect with just
  • Present perfect with yet
  • Present perfect with already
Confidential Dossier — Maya Lin

Secret Information:

• Your sources confirm that engineers have just discovered suspicious code alterations in the system core.

• The Transport Ministry has already demanded a full congressional inquiry.

• However, corporate officials have not released the diagnostic logs yet.

Required Production Prompts:

"The Ministry has already intervened, but the board hasn't published the reports yet!"

Student 2 • Class 39 Focus

Dr. Aris Thorne

Chief Systems Historian

Persona: A detail-oriented analyst who chronologically reconstructs systems anomalies.

Mission: Explain the exact step-by-step sequence of events using adverbs of time.

Target Language Checklist:
  • Adverbs of time: Initially, Prior to
  • Sequencing: Subsequently, Meanwhile
  • Resolution: Eventually
Confidential Dossier — Dr. Thorne

Chronological Event Timeline:

• Initially, power levels remained stable at 100%.

• Prior to the 08:15 surge, telemetry registered voltage spikes at 07:50.

• Subsequently, main thrusters failed; meanwhile, backup power lagged by 40 seconds.

• Eventually, automated safety locks initiated full shutdown.

Required Production Prompts:

"Prior to the shutdown, voltage spiked; subsequently, the backup failure triggered full locking."

Student 3 • Class 59 Focus

Elena Vance

VP of Public Relations & Risk

Persona: A strategic corporate spokesperson analyzing behavioral norms and exceptions.

Mission: Explain typical leadership behavior while highlighting recent anomalous actions.

Target Language Checklist:
  • Typical habits: typically, tends to
  • Contrast: whereas, on the other hand
  • Exceptions: with the exception of
Confidential Dossier — Elena Vance

Behavioral & Corporate Analysis:

• Chief Engineer Vance typically adheres strictly to safety protocols, with the exception of yesterday's override.

• CEO Sterling tends to hold immediate press conferences during crises; whereas today she remains silent.

• On the other hand, the engineering board has praised the automated failsafe.

Required Production Prompts:

"Our team typically follows protocol, with the exception of this isolated glitch; whereas the CEO..."
