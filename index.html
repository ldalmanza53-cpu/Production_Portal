A1 Speaking Production Task

Project Horizon: Eco-Resort Launch Summit

Collaborative Mission 4 Roles

Main Task: Build the Eco-Resort Launch Plan

You are the executive team of Horizon Eco-Resort. Today, you must hold a planning meeting to decide where to build the resort, arrange the grand opening party, and set future green goals for the next year.

Final Group Deliverable

Complete the Resort Launch Agreement in the Scratchpad with: 1 chosen island, 2 gala event requests, and 3 future objectives for next year.

4 Mission Stages

  1. Stage 1: Compare islands & choose location (Alex leads).
  2. Stage 2: Request & arrange the Gala Event (Morgan leads).
  3. Stage 3: Present future objectives & goals (Taylor & Jordan lead).
  4. Stage 4: Sign consensus in the scratchpad.

Class 62 Focus

Alex

Location Specialist

You are the venue expert. You have data comparing two island locations for the eco-resort.

Alex's Secret Data

Island Alpha: Hotter ($32^\circ\text{C}$), larger, $150\text{ km}$ from airport. Has the highest mountain ($800\text{ m}$).

Island Beta: Cooler ($25^\circ\text{C}$), smaller, $30\text{ km}$ from airport. More peaceful than Alpha.

Target Checklist (Class 62)

  • Compare places ($taller, hotter$).
  • Use superlatives ($the\ most\ peaceful$).
  • Ask facts ($How\ high?, How\ far?$).
Class 67 Focus

Morgan

Gala Coordinator

You plan the Grand Opening Gala. You must make arrangements and polite requests to the team.

Morgan's Secret Data

Gala Requirements: You need organic food catering, live acoustic music, and a large outdoor stage.

Constraint: You must make polite requests to Alex, Taylor, and Jordan for help.

Target Checklist (Class 67)

  • Make polite requests ($Can\ you...?, Could\ we...?).
  • Express needs ($I\ would\ like\ to\ request...).
  • Arrange event details ($We\ are\ arranging...).
Class 69 Focus

Taylor

Future Innovation Lead

You manage high-tech green features. You present future plans using be going to and Present Continuous.

Taylor's Secret Data

Future Goals: Next month, you are installing 500 solar panels. Next year, you are going to launch electric boats for guests.

Expectation: You expect 100% solar power by December.

Target Checklist (Class 69)

  • Express goals with be going to.
  • State scheduled plans with Present Continuous.
  • Explain future expectations clearly.
Class 69 Focus

Jordan

Sustainability Director

You manage eco-certifications and guest experiences. You explain future objectives to the team.

Jordan's Secret Data

Future Goals: You are opening an organic farm next summer. You are going to protect the local coral reef.

Expectation: You expect 5,000 eco-tourists during the first season.

Target Checklist (Class 69)

  • Use be going to for future objectives.
  • Use Present Continuous for future dates.
  • Share future expectations with the team.
