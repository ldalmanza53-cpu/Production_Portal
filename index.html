The Grand Hotel

Mission: Open The Hotel!

You are a team. You have a new hotel, but you have no plan! You must talk together to plan the jobs, the prices, and the schedules.

Step 1

Click your card. Read your secret goal. Do not show your card.

Step 2

Talk to your team. Ask questions. Answer questions.

Step 3

Agree on the details. Write the plan in the notebook below.

Final Deliverable: Write 3 Jobs, 3 Prices, and 3 Schedules in the Notebook.

The Manager

Class 5 • You plan the jobs.

Top Secret

You need to know everyone's job. You need people to work at the front desk and clean.

  • Ask: "What do you do?"
  • Ask: "Where do you work?"
  • Goal: Find out 2 jobs for the notebook.

The Receptionist

Class 5 • You talk to guests.

Top Secret

You answer questions about your job. You want a desk job.

  • Say: "I am a receptionist."
  • Say: "I work at the front desk."
  • Goal: Refuse to do any cleaning jobs.

The Guide

Class 8 • You describe everyone.

Top Secret

You listen to the team. You must describe what other people do.

  • Say: "He is a..." / "She is a..."
  • Say: "He works in..." / "She works in..."
  • Goal: Describe 2 people's jobs clearly for the notebook.

The Accountant

Class 10 • You control the money.

Top Secret

You need to set prices for a hotel room and breakfast. You want cheap options.

  • Ask: "How much is this?"
  • Say: "That is 50 dollars."
  • Goal: Do not agree to a price over 100 dollars.

Head Cleaner

Class 25 • You plan routines.

Top Secret

You want a clean hotel, but you are very tired. You want other people to clean.

  • Ask: "How often do you clean?"
  • Say: "I never clean the windows."
  • Goal: Tell the team you "sometimes" work, but make someone else work "always".

The Chef

Class 25 • You cook food.

Top Secret

You love your job, but you hate getting up early. You only cook breakfast.

  • Say: "I usually cook eggs."
  • Say: "I always work in the morning."
  • Goal: Refuse to cook dinner. Say: "I never work at night."

Pedagogical Objectives

Target Profile

Extracted Syllabus Objectives

  • Class 5 (Manager & Receptionist): "Ask and answer questions about different types of jobs"
  • Class 8 (The Guide): "Watch in context how people describe their jobs (Video)" (Adapted for communicative speaking: describing 3rd person jobs)
  • Class 10 (The Accountant): "Ask for and give prices"
  • Class 25 (Cleaner & Chef): "Validate previous knowledge about how to describe the frequency of certain activities (Grammar Plus)"
