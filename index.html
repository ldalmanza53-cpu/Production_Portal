NovaWave Startup

B1 Level Task • 5 Members

The Startup Hiring Crisis

The Scenario

Your tech startup, NovaWave, is growing fast. You desperately need to hire new people, but you only have the budget to hire ONE person right now. The team has different opinions on which career choice is the best for the company's future.

Your Final Deliverable

As a team, you must:

  • Agree on exactly ONE job to hire.
  • Write a formal request to the CEO in the Scratchpad to ask for the remaining budget.

Mission Stages

  1. Discuss: Talk about the pros and cons of the different jobs (Use gerunds).
  2. Negotiate: Make polite requests to each other to compromise (Use modals).
  3. Discover: Ask indirect questions to find out secret information from HR.

Budget Director

Your Rule: Make requests with different levels of formality to stop them from spending money.

Target Language (Class 10):

HR Investigator

Secret Motive: The CEO told you: "We must hire someone who can work on weekends." You need to find out which job requires this by asking indirect questions.

Your Rule: Do not ask direct questions. Report what the CEO said.

Target Language (Class 12):
